How the automation works

Never miss a lead again

A customer fills in your website form while you're on a job. Here's what happens next, automatically:

Website form→ Enquiry sorted (urgent / normal / quote)→ Saved to your lead sheet→ You get an instant alert→ Customer gets a reply→ Follow-up reminder

Urgent jobs are always flagged for you to handle personally. The system only helps you respond faster; important decisions stay with you.
